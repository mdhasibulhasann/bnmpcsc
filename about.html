Lt. Col. Md. Tajul Islam, M.Phil, PhD, AEC, Principal of Birshreshtha Noor Mohammad Public College, serves as the Chief Patron of BNMPC Science Club.

Under his valued guidance, encouragement and visionary direction, the club continues to foster scientific curiosity, creativity and innovation among students. The BNMPC National Science Carnival 2026 is being organised in alignment with this vision—providing young minds with a platform to explore ideas, demonstrate their talents and participate in meaningful scientific activities.

With the continued support and leadership of our Chief Patron, BNMPC Science Club is moving forward with confidence, creating new opportunities and inspiring the next generation of scientists and innovators.
